## Viewer

The viewer combines case records, register of actions entries, parties,
attorneys, documents, calendars, payments, tentative rulings, and derived
research fields. Availability varies by case and source. Derived tags,
classifications, entity matches, and inferred outcomes are research aids and
are not court findings.

SFSC is an independent public record archive. It is not affiliated with or
operated by the San Francisco Superior Court.

## Case Record Archive

Civil, criminal, probate, family, and other case records grouped by case-number prefix and scan state. Completed means every listed public document has archived bytes; core means the essential document set is captured; partial means some document bytes are captured; docket only means docket or summary rows are captured but document bytes are not; docket complete means the public docket has no document bytes to capture. Indexed, discovered, no-match, and restricted rows are visible terminal, backlog, or access states, not hidden records.
